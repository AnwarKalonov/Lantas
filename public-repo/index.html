The loop

Ask. Watch.
Take over.

No hidden queue and no black box. You see the step, the tool and the result while it happens, and one key takes the wheel back.

Read how it works →
  1. 01
    Say what you want

    One sentence is enough. Lantas picks the surface and the tools, and asks only when it genuinely needs a detail.

  2. 02
    Watch the work

    Reads, edits, clicks and checks appear as steps with real output. The blue pointer shows where it is on a page or on your Mac.

  3. 03
    Take over any time

    Type, scroll or press Esc. The run pauses, Lantas re-reads the changed state, and continues from where you left it.

Computer control

It can use your Mac.
You can take it back.

Lantas reads the accessibility tree of the front window, draws a private labelled grid over a screenshot, and acts with the real pointer and keyboard. Only the blue pointer is drawn on your display; the picture stays private.

  • ✓Click, type, scroll, drag and switch windows by control name
  • ✓One Control switch shared by the composer, browser and screen card
  • ✓Any real input pauses the run; nothing continues behind your back
